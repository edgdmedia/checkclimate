# Logos

The Check Climate mark is a sun with hand-drawn rays around a ring and a check mark over an amber disc. The wordmark is CHECK above CLIMATE in a wide geometric sans. The inks are exact brand tokens: `brand-forest` #2C572A, `brand-amber` #FFB600, `brand-deep` #003C25 and white.

| File | What | Use on |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `check-climate-lockup.png` (600 × 200) | Primary lockup: mark + CHECK in forest, CLIMATE in amber | White or `surface-muted`. The site header, at 40px tall. |
| `check-climate-lockup-reverse.png` (900 × 300, transparent) | **Dark-mode lockup**: white rays and white CHECK, `brand-deep` CLIMATE | Dark mode (`surface-page` #212121, `surface-raised` #1A1A1A) and dark photography. Never on white, where the white parts vanish. |
| `check-climate-mark.png` (500 × 500, transparent) | Mark alone, forest rays, white check | Light grounds: favicons, avatars, social profile images. |
| `check-climate-mark-white-bg.jpg` (500 × 500) | The same mark on a white square | Places that need an opaque square, such as app tiles and the WordPress site icon. |
| `check-climate-mark-on-forest.jpg` (500 × 500) | Reversed mark (white rays and ring, forest check) on a `brand-forest` square | Social avatars and coloured contexts that need a square. |
| `check-climate-mark-simple.png` (500 × 500, transparent) | **Dark-mode mark**: ring and check, no rays | The icon on dark grounds, and at small sizes (32px and under) where the rays fill in. |

Rules:
- Never redraw, recolour, stretch or re-set the wordmark in type. Red Hat Display is **not** the logo face.
- Keep clear space of at least half the mark's ring diameter on every side.
- The lockup should be at least 32px tall on screen. Below that, use the simple mark.
- **Light mode:** `check-climate-lockup.png` + `check-climate-mark.png`. **Dark mode:** `check-climate-lockup-reverse.png` + `check-climate-mark-simple.png`. Swap them with the theme, never show both.
- On #212121 the `brand-deep` CLIMATE in the reverse lockup is low-contrast (about 1.4:1). The white sun and CHECK carry the logo. Keep the lockup at 40px tall or larger in dark mode.
